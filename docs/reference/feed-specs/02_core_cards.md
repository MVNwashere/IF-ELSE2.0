# Core Cards: The 10 Most Useful Cards

These are the cards that matter to almost every customer. They sit at the top of the feed most of the time, and only make way when something urgent turns up (for example a suspicious payment).

Every card has two looks:

- **In the feed (not clicked):** what the customer sees while scrolling. It should be readable at a glance and tell the main story in a few seconds.
- **When clicked:** what opens when the customer taps the card. It goes deeper, and at the bottom shows a row of **related cards** that follow from what they just looked at.

Every card also has a small "⋯" menu in the top corner with "Not relevant to me", "Show me less of this" and "Why am I seeing this?".

---

## CARD 1: Your Money (Accounts & Net Worth)

**What it is:** The "where do I stand" card. It shows the total amount of money the customer has, and what it is made of.

**Who it fits:** Everyone. It is the first or second card in every feed.

**In the feed (not clicked)**
- A large card with a wallet icon and the title "Your money".
- One big number in the middle: the total amount (e.g. € 48,215.30).
- Under it, a small green or red line showing the change this month (e.g. "▲ € 1,120 this month").
- A thin stacked bar under the number, split in colours: current accounts, savings, investments, pension, loans. Below it, a short legend with the amounts.
- A faint line in the background showing how the total moved over the last 12 months.
- A small "eye" icon to hide all amounts on screen.

**When clicked**
- Opens a full overview page. At the top: the same total, now with a bigger 12-month chart and a switch between 1 month, 6 months and 1 year.
- Below it, every account as a row: name, type, balance and a small note such as "2 payments pending".
- Tapping a colour in the bar filters the list to only that type (e.g. only savings).
- Quick buttons at the top: Transfer, Pay, Top up.
- At the bottom: related cards (Monthly Spending, Savings Goals, Investments, Financial Health).

**How it looks for different personas**
- **TEEN / STU:** Shows just "Available to spend" as one friendly number. No net worth, no bar.
- **SEN:** Very large text, only current account and savings, no chart.
- **INV:** Adds a line such as "62% of your money is invested".

## CARD 2: Spending This Month

**What it is:** Shows where the money went this month and whether the customer is on track compared with a normal month.

**Who it fits:** Everyone, but especially STU, YPRO, PAR and SELF. It moves higher at the end of the month and right after payday.

**In the feed (not clicked)**
- A large card with a chart icon and the title "Spending this month".
- Headline: "€ 1,842 spent so far, you usually spend € 2,300". Under it a progress bar that fills up and turns amber, then red, when spending gets close to or above the usual amount.
- A small donut chart with the top categories (groceries, housing, transport, dining, shopping) and their amounts.
- One sentence of insight, e.g. "Dining is 34% higher than usual".
- Two small chips: "9 days until payday" and "Safe to spend today: € 42".

**When clicked**
- A full page with a month selector at the top (swipe to go back to earlier months).
- A bigger donut chart, then a list of all categories sorted from most to least spending. Each category can be opened to show the individual payments in it.
- A comparison view: this month vs last month vs average, per category.
- A "Set a budget" section where the customer picks a monthly limit per category and sees it as a bar.
- Related cards at the bottom: Unusual Activity, Coming Up, Savings Goals, Subscription Audit.

**How it looks for different personas**
- **TEEN:** "You spent € 24 of your pocket money", playful and simple.
- **SELF:** A switch to see business spending and private spending separately.
- **PAR:** A "household" view when a partner is linked.

## CARD 3: Coming Up (Upcoming Payments & Bills)

**What it is:** A look ahead at what is about to leave the account, so nothing comes as a surprise.

**Who it fits:** Everyone. It is pushed higher when a large payment is near or when the balance looks too low.

**In the feed (not clicked)**
- A card with a calendar icon and the title "Coming up". In the corner a total, e.g. "€ 612 in the next 7 days".
- A list of the next 3 to 5 payments. Each row has a small logo or coloured letter, the name, the date ("Tomorrow", "Fri 3 Oct") and the amount. Payments the bank expects but which aren't fixed yet have a dotted outline.
- When the balance may not be enough, an amber strip appears: "Your balance may be short by € 85 on 14 Oct" with a "Top up from savings" button.
- If nothing is coming: "Nothing due in the next 7 days 🎉".

**When clicked**
- A month calendar with dots on the days that have payments. Tapping a day shows its payments below.
- A switch between "Going out" and "Coming in" (salary, benefits, invoices paid to the customer).
- Tapping a single payment opens its page: who it is to, how often it repeats, the history of past amounts and buttons such as "Change date", "Pause" or "Cancel subscription".
- Related cards: Unusual Activity, Your Cards, Savings Goals, Subscription Audit.

**How it looks for different personas**
- **SEN:** Only 3 rows, in plain words ("on Friday"), with big amounts.
- **SELF:** Also shows invoices the customer is waiting to receive, in green.

## CARD 4: Unusual Activity

**What it is:** The "hey, look at this" card. It only appears when something odd happens: a bill that jumped up, a double charge, a payment in another country, a salary that didn't arrive, or a possible fraud attempt.

**Who it fits:** Everyone. An urgent one jumps to the top of the feed.

**Types of alert (each has its own icon and colour)**
- Price increase ("Your energy bill went up € 38 (22%)")
- Double charge ("You were charged twice at FitClub today")
- New monthly charge ("New monthly charge: StreamBox € 12.99. Was this you?")
- Unusually high payment
- Card used in a new country
- Salary or pension hasn't arrived
- Suspected fraud ("Suspicious payment of € 299")

**In the feed (not clicked)**
- An amber card (red for fraud) with a matching icon and a short title.
- One sentence in plain language, and the amount in big type.
- A simple side-by-side comparison when relevant: "Usually € 172 → Now € 210".
- Two buttons at the bottom: "Yes, that was me" and "Something's wrong". For price increases: "See details" and "Switch provider".
- If there are several alerts, they are grouped in one taller card listing up to three.

**When clicked**
- A page with the full detail: merchant, date, time, location, the card used, and a small history of earlier payments to the same merchant.
- For price changes: a bar chart of the last 12 months.
- For fraud: a big red "Block my card" button at the top and a "Call us" button underneath.
- Related cards: Your Cards, Coming Up, Spending This Month, contact and support.

**How it looks for different personas**
- **SEN:** Bigger text and a phone number to call shown directly on the card.
- **TEEN:** Also notifies the parent view, with a simpler message.

## CARD 5: Insurance

**What it is:** One place showing which insurances the customer has, when they renew and where there may be gaps.

**Who it fits:** Everyone except TEEN. Strongest for PAR, HOME, SELF and SEN. It becomes prominent about 6 weeks before a renewal, and gets more urgent at two weeks and three days.

**In the feed (not clicked)**
- A card with a shield icon and the title "Insurance". A status badge: "Renews in 12 days" (amber), "All active" (green) or "Gap found" (blue).
- When a renewal is close: a headline such as "Car insurance renews on 14 Oct", the yearly price (€ 684) and the change compared to last year (▲ € 42).
- A horizontal row of small icons: Car, Home, Health, Life, Travel, Liability. A tick means covered; a dashed outline means not covered.
- One line of insight, e.g. "Similar cover available from € 612/year" or "You have no liability insurance".
- Buttons: "Review & compare" and "Remind me later".

**When clicked**
- A list of every insurance: insurer, yearly price, renewal date, and a short summary of what it covers.
- Each policy can be opened for more detail, documents and a "Make a claim" shortcut.
- A comparison section showing alternative offers side by side with yearly price and key differences.
- A "Gaps" section explaining in plain words what isn't covered and whether it matters for this customer.
- Related cards: Car Costs, Travel Insurance Check, Life Insurance Gap, Coming Up.

**How it looks for different personas**
- **PAR:** Highlights life insurance and family liability.
- **HOME:** Highlights building and contents insurance.
- **STU:** Shows cheap starter options for liability and contents.
- **SEN:** Large text and a phone number for help.

## CARD 6: Your Goals (Savings Goals)

**What it is:** Turns saving into concrete goals ("Summer trip", "Emergency fund", "New phone").

**Who it fits:** Everyone. Strongest for TEEN, STU, YPRO and PAR.

**In the feed (not clicked)**
- A card with a target icon and the title "Your goals", with a badge like "2 on track".
- Up to three goals, each with an emoji, a name, a progress ring or bar, "€ 1,250 of € 2,000" and a status ("On track", "Behind by € 80", "Done 🎉").
- A helpful line: "Save € 60 more per month to reach 'Summer trip' by June".
- A button "Add to a goal" and a smaller "New goal".
- If the customer has no goals yet: "What are you saving for?" with four tappable suggestions: Holiday, Emergency fund, New phone, Car.

**When clicked**
- A page listing all goals. Each goal opens into its own page with a growth chart, the money added so far, and the date it will likely be reached.
- A quick "Add money" sheet with amount buttons (€10, €25, €50, your own).
- Options to save automatically: monthly amount, or round up every card payment.
- Related cards: Round-Up Saving, Emergency Fund Builder, Investments (for goals longer than three years), Spending This Month.

**How it looks for different personas**
- **TEEN:** Playful progress animations and badges.
- **PAR:** Often called "Kids' fund" or "Family holiday".
- **SEN:** Renamed "Money set aside", with simple bars.

## CARD 7: Investments

**What it is:** A snapshot of the customer's investments and how they're doing. For those who don't invest yet, a soft introduction to getting started.

**Who it fits:** INV, HOME, PRE and YPRO (for those who hold investments). The "getting started" version is shown to YPRO, PAR and HOME with spare savings.

**In the feed (not clicked) for someone who invests**
- A large card with a trending icon, the title "Investments" and a badge "Markets open" or "Closed".
- Big number with the portfolio value (€ 27,430) and today's change (+ € 112, 0.41%) in green or red.
- A small line chart with buttons 1D, 1M, 1Y, All.
- Top three holdings with name, value and change.
- A small bar showing the split between stocks, bonds, funds and cash.
- Buttons "Open portfolio" and "Add money", and a small line "Past performance is no guarantee of future results."

**In the feed (not clicked) for someone who doesn't invest yet**
- A calmer card: "Your € 6,200 savings earn very little. Start from € 25 a month."
- Three simple starter options, each with a risk scale from 1 to 7.
- A button "Learn how investing works".

**When clicked**
- Full portfolio page: bigger chart, all holdings with value, share of the total and performance, and a view by type, region or sector.
- A section with the money added vs the growth.
- For the starter version: a short, friendly explainer in three steps and the three starter options in more detail.
- Related cards: Market Moves for My Holdings, Idle Cash Warning, Your Money, Your Goals.

## CARD 8: What You Owe (Loans, Mortgage & Credit)

**What it is:** A clear overview of all debt: how much is left, what it costs, and when it will be paid off.

**Who it fits:** STU (student loan, overdraft), YPRO, PAR, HOME, SELF, PRE. Customers without any debt never see this card.

**In the feed (not clicked)**
- A card with a building or credit-card icon and the title "What you owe". A badge warns when a mortgage rate is about to change ("Rate resets in 2 months").
- Big number: total outstanding (€ 212,400) and the total monthly payment (€ 1,134).
- A row per debt (Mortgage, Car loan, Student loan, Credit card, Overdraft) with amount left, interest rate, monthly amount and a small bar showing how much is repaid.
- An insight line, e.g. "You have paid off 28% of your mortgage" or "Paying € 100 extra a month saves € 3,100 interest".
- Buttons "See details" and "Try an extra payment".
- An amber badge "High usage" if a credit card or overdraft is almost full.

**When clicked**
- A page showing a chart of how the debt shrinks over time until payoff date.
- A slider: "What if I pay extra each month?" showing interest saved and years earlier.
- For mortgages: the current rate versus rates on offer today.
- Related cards: Mortgage Rate Reset, Refinancing Opportunity, Spending This Month, Financial Health.

**How it looks for different personas**
- **STU:** Only student loan and overdraft, in a friendly tone.
- **PRE:** Focus on the payoff date and what will be free afterwards.

## CARD 9: Your Cards

**What it is:** A quick control panel for the customer's payment cards.

**Who it fits:** Everyone. Top priority for SEN, for parents of TEEN accounts, and for people who travel.

**In the feed (not clicked)**
- A card with a card icon and the title "Your cards", and a badge "All secure" (green) or "Attention" (amber).
- A swipeable row of card pictures (debit, credit, virtual), each with the last four digits and a status such as "Active", "Frozen" or "Expires 11/26".
- Under the selected card, four switches: Freeze, Online payments, Abroad payments, Contactless.
- A bar showing today's spending against the daily limit.
- A red "Report lost or stolen" link, always visible.
- If the card expires soon: "New card on its way" or an "Order replacement" button.

**When clicked**
- A page for that card with bigger controls: change daily and monthly limits, see PIN options, see the card's recent payments, add to phone wallet.
- Delivery tracking for a replacement card.
- Related cards: Unusual Activity, Card Abroad Settings, Phishing Watch.

**How it looks for different personas**
- **TEEN:** The teen sees limits but can't change them. The parent sees the same card with edit buttons.
- **SEN:** The "Report lost or stolen" link becomes a large red button with a phone number.

## CARD 10: Financial Health

**What it is:** A personal score from 0 to 100 and the single most useful next step.

**Who it fits:** Everyone except TEEN (a simplified version for STU).

**In the feed (not clicked)**
- A card with a heart-pulse icon, the title "Financial health" and a badge "Good", "Fair" or "Needs attention".
- A half-circle gauge with the score in the middle (e.g. 72) and "+3 since last month".
- Four small pills with mini bars: Spending, Saving, Borrowing, Planning.
- A highlighted box with the next best step, e.g. "Build your emergency fund: you can cover 1.2 months, aim for 3." with a button "Start a goal".
- A small link "How is this calculated?".

**When clicked**
- A page with the gauge and a line chart of the score over the last months.
- Each of the four areas opens into a short, friendly explanation of what helps or hurts the score, with one suggested action each.
- A "Next steps" list of three to five actions sorted from biggest to smallest impact, each with a button that starts it.
- Tone is always encouraging, never shaming.
- Related cards: Your Goals, Insurance, Investments, What You Owe.
