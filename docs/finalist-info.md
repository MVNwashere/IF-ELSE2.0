# Tectonic Hackathon: Final Round Info

Source: email from Tectonic Hackathon (hackathon@tectonicconf.eu), "You made the final. Confirm as soon as possible."

## Summary

Team If/Else made the top 32 of the 280 teams that submitted on 30 September (seven cities, same evening). The final round is **Tuesday 20 October 2026 in Ghent**: seven hours of hacking on harder cases from **SD Worx** and **KBC**, then semi-finals and a grand final with a **€10,000** main cash prize (powered by Spott). It takes place the day before Tectonic opens.

## Where and when

- **When:** Tuesday 20 October 2026, check-in and breakfast from 08:00
- **Where:** De Vooruit, Sint-Pietersnieuwstraat 23, 9000 Ghent (semi-finals at Wintercircus)
- **Bring:** laptops, chargers and your whole team. Breakfast and lunch are provided.

## Schedule

| Time  | Event        | Details                                                      |
|-------|--------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| 08:00 | Check in     | Breakfast at De Vooruit. Briefings at 08:45.                 |
| 09:00 | Hack         | Seven hours on cases from SD Worx and KBC. **Submit by 16:00.** |
| 16:15 | Semi-finals  | Pitch at Wintercircus. The best team per track goes through. |
| 18:00 | Grand final  | On stage at De Vooruit for €10,000.                          |
| 21:15 | Tecnotonic   | Party at Club Wintercircus. Finalists are on the list.       |

## Other notes

- Spots had to be confirmed by 14 October 2026, or they went to the waitlist.
