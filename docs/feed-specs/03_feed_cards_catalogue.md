# Feed Card Catalogue: 100 Targeted Cards

These cards live in the scrollable feed. Unlike the 10 core cards, each one is shown only to **specific personas or situations**. Persona codes are the ones from `01_personas.md`:

`TEEN` · `STU` · `YPRO` · `PAR` · `HOME` · `SELF` · `INV` · `PRE` · `SEN`

## How to read each card

Every card below has:

- **What it is:** the purpose of the card in one line.
- **Fits:** which personas see it, and in which situation.
- **In the feed (not clicked):** what the customer sees while scrolling.
- **When clicked:** what opens after a tap, including the related cards shown at the bottom of that page.

**Card types**

| Type | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Action | A personal alert that asks the customer to do something |
| Insight | An observation about the customer's own money, nothing to do |
| Product | A gentle suggestion of a bank product, never pushy, always dismissible |
| Editorial | A "news" or tips article. Tapping it opens the article and then related cards |
| Tool | An interactive calculator or planner |

**Shared look for all cards:** rounded card, small icon and title at the top, a "⋯" menu with "Not relevant to me" and "Why am I seeing this?". Editorial cards have a picture on top. Cards for TEEN are colourful and playful; cards for SEN use big text, high contrast and plain words. Nothing about credit or investing is ever shown to TEEN, and only sparingly to SEN.

## A. Youth & Student (10 cards)

### YS-01 · Pocket Money Countdown
**Type:** Insight · **Fits:** TEEN, right after pocket money arrives
**What it is:** Helps a teen see how long their pocket money will last.
**In the feed:** A bright card with "€ 14 left, 6 days until your next pocket money". A coloured bar shows how fast the money is going (green, then orange, then red). A friendly emoji reacts to the situation.
**When clicked:** A simple page showing where the pocket money went (snacks, games, transport) as colourful bars, plus a tip such as "Spend € 2 a day to make it last". Related cards: First Savings Challenge, Saving for That Thing.

### YS-02 · First Savings Challenge
**Type:** Action · **Fits:** TEEN with no savings goal yet
**What it is:** A small, fun challenge that starts the saving habit.
**In the feed:** "Save € 20 in 4 weeks": four empty circles, one per week, that fill with stamps. A reward badge is shown greyed-out. Button "Accept challenge".
**When clicked:** Explains the challenge in three short steps, lets the teen pick the goal (phone, concert, bike) and shows the badge they will unlock. Related cards: Money Quiz of the Week, Saving for That Thing.

### YS-03 · Money Quiz of the Week
**Type:** Editorial · **Fits:** TEEN, STU
**What it is:** A short weekly quiz that teaches a money basic.
**In the feed:** A card with a question mark icon, the topic ("What is interest?") and a streak counter ("3 weeks in a row 🔥").
**When clicked:** Three multiple-choice questions, one at a time, with instant feedback and a short explanation after each. Ends with a score and points. Related cards: Saving for That Thing, Your First Tax Return.

### YS-04 · Saving for That Thing
**Type:** Insight · **Fits:** TEEN with a saving goal
**What it is:** A picture-based goal tracker for something the teen wants.
**In the feed:** A picture or emoji of the item, its price, a filling progress bar and the line "You'll have it by 12 March if you keep going".
**When clicked:** A bigger progress view with a "Add € 5" quick button, the history of deposits, and a cheer when milestones (25%, 50%, 75%) are reached. Related card: First Savings Challenge.

### YS-05 · Ask Parent to Raise Limit
**Type:** Action · **Fits:** TEEN who often hits their daily limit
**What it is:** A polite way to ask a parent for a higher spending limit.
**In the feed:** "You hit your limit twice this month. Want to ask your parent for more?" with a single button "Send request".
**When clicked:** A small form: pick the new limit and write a short reason. After sending, it shows "Waiting for your parent" and later the answer. Related card for the parent: Teen's First Bank Card.

### YS-06 · Student Survival Countdown
**Type:** Insight · **Fits:** STU with low balance and many days until income
**What it is:** Turns a low balance into a simple daily budget.
**In the feed:** "€ 186 left for 11 days = € 17 a day". A gauge coloured green, orange or red. Two small tip chips: "Cook at home", "Skip takeaway".
**When clicked:** A day-by-day view of how the money could be spread, the biggest spending leaks this month, and a few cheap-living tips. Related cards: Student Discount Finder, Cheaper Phone Plan & Subscriptions.

### YS-07 · Student Discount Finder
**Type:** Product · **Fits:** STU with verified student status
**What it is:** Shows discounts students can use.
**In the feed:** Three small tiles side by side (transport, software, food), each with a "% off" label and an "Activate" button.
**When clicked:** The full list of discounts by category, with how to claim each one. Related cards: Student Survival Countdown, Cheaper Phone Plan & Subscriptions.

### YS-08 · Rent Deposit Explained
**Type:** Editorial · **Fits:** STU, right after paying a rent deposit
**What it is:** A guide for renting as a student.
**In the feed:** Picture of a key with the title "Getting your deposit back: 6 things to do at move-out" and "4 min read".
**When clicked:** The article with six numbered tips and a checklist (photos at move-in, reading the contract, final meter readings). Related cards: Renting vs Buying Calculator, Moving House Checklist.

### YS-09 · Cheaper Phone Plan & Subscriptions
**Type:** Insight · **Fits:** STU, YPRO
**What it is:** Shows when the customer pays more than people like them for phone and streaming.
**In the feed:** "You pay € 47 a month for phone and streaming. People your age pay € 31". Two suggested cuts underneath with the saving next to each.
**When clicked:** A list of each subscription with its price and how it compares, plus simple steps to switch or cancel. Related cards: Subscription Audit.

### YS-10 · Your First Tax Return
**Type:** Editorial · **Fits:** STU, YPRO with a part-time job, in tax season
**What it is:** A friendly intro to taxes for people with their first side job.
**In the feed:** "Working part-time? You may get money back". A small checklist preview and an estimated refund range.
**When clicked:** The full guide: what documents are needed, what can be deducted, and deadlines. Related cards: Tax Return Checklist.
